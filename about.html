About DocuLearn

A localized learning platform for AP10.

Purpose

DocuLearn is a learning platform for Grade 10 Araling Panlipunan (AP10) students of Guimba. It brings together localized materials, existing documentaries, and an interactive forum to help students explore contemporary issues in their own community.

Features

  • Module-based lessons on contemporary issues
  • Localized materials from Guimba (photos, data, records)
  • Curated documentaries from YouTube
  • Discussion forum for questions and reflections
  • Student progress tracking
